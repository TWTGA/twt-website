Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

Think With Technology is a Melbourne based automation and consulting business. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Think With Technology. You can contact us at info@thinkwithtechnology.com.au.

What we collect

When you book a call with us, we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, phone number and any notes you add. If you email or message us, we also keep what you send.

How we use it

We use your details only to arrange and run your call, reply to your enquiries, and provide any services you ask for. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

We use trusted third party services to run the business, including Calendly (bookings), Microsoft 365 and Outlook (email and calendar), and Zapier (to connect these tools). Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. They have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and website visitors

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our website host may record basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running securely.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or provide our services, and as required for business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us at info@thinkwithtechnology.com.au and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
